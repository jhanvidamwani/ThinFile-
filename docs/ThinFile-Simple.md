# ThinFile

### Don't build credit. Prove it.

---

## The idea in one sentence

ThinFile turns your bank history into a simple **proof you pay on time**, which you can share with any lender or landlord as a link.

---

## The problem

Meet **Priya**. She moved to the US 18 months ago for grad school.
She has paid rent on time every single month. Phone bill, on time. Electricity, on time.

She applies for a credit card. **Declined.**
She applies for an apartment. **Declined.**

Why? She has no credit score. To the system, she doesn't exist.

There are millions of people like Priya: new immigrants, international students, gig workers, young people. They're responsible with money, and nobody can see it.

---

## What ThinFile does

**3 steps. 2 minutes.**

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Connect your bank** | One tap. Read-only. We never move money. |
| **2. We find your proof** | *"18 of 18 rent payments on time. 52 of 52 bills on time. Never overdrawn."* |
| **3. Share your link** | Send your **Reliability Report** to any lender or landlord. |

That's it. No waiting months. No special apps for paying rent. No asking your landlord to sign up for anything.

---

## What makes it different

### 1. Proof, not a score
Other apps say *"wait 6 months and your score might go up."*
ThinFile says *"you already have a track record. Here it is."*

### 2. Fair by design
ThinFile **never sees your name, where you're from, or what you look like.** It only looks at how you pay.
We prove it live: same bank history, 5 different names → **the exact same result, every time.**

### 3. A "not yet" instead of a "no"
If you're not ready yet, ThinFile tells you **exactly what to fix**:
> *"Avoid overdrafts for the next 60 days and you'll qualify."*

Nobody else tells people how to get to yes.

## How it works (the simple version)

A small team of AI helpers looks at your bank history, each with one job:

1. **The Privacy Guard** hides your name and personal details first.
2. **The Sorter** labels every payment: rent, bills, income, spending.
3. **The Checker** counts how often you paid on time.
4. **The Calculator** gives a fixed score. Same history, same score, always. No guessing.
5. **The Coach** tells you what to fix if you're not there yet.

Every step is shown on screen. Nothing is hidden.

## If judges ask…

| Question | Simple answer |
| --- | --- |
| *"Can the AI make things up?"* | No. The AI sorts and explains. The score is fixed math: same history, same result. |
| *"Is it really fair?"* | It never sees names or backgrounds. We show it live: 5 names, 1 result. |
